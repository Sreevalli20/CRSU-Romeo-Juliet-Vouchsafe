Research & Methodology

Policy architecture and experimental design

Key Innovations

  • Constraint-First Design: Hard constraints are checked before any scoring. No compatibility score can override consent or explicit constraints.
  • Uncertainty-Aware Scoring: Missing information is treated as uncertainty, not as compatibility or incompatibility. Uncertainty is explicitly penalized.
  • Value-of-Information Clarification: Questions are asked only when expected information gain exceeds a threshold, not merely because data is missing.
  • Delayed Feedback Learning: The policy updates its understanding based on feedback that arrives after decisions, preserving temporal correctness.
  • Global Allocation: Pairs are selected to maximize total value across the pool, not just individual pair ranking.

Limitations

  • Higher ask cost (684 vs 200 for greedy) due to conservative clarification
  • Sparse variant performance is weak (0.0 MSMI) - geographic fragmentation challenging
  • Learning component is simple - could benefit from more sophisticated bandit methods
  • Value-of-information threshold is heuristic - not tuned on validation data
  • Results are on synthetic data only - not evidence of real-world performance

Ethics & Safety

Consent-first design principles

🔒

Consent-First

Hard constraints are never overridden. Age, gender, relationship structure, smoking, children, and geographic preferences are strictly enforced. No score can compensate for a constraint violation.

👤

Human Authority

The policy provides decision support, not decisions. A human operator must approve any real-world introduction. The system does not contact people directly or make commitments.

❓

Uncertainty Transparency

Missing information is explicitly acknowledged. The policy distinguishes between unknown, declined, and observed. Uncertainty is visible in scoring and explanation.

🎯

Data Minimization

Only information necessary for decision-making is requested. Clarification is selective based on expected value, not blanket data collection.

🧪

Synthetic Only

All profiles, conversations, and outcomes in this evaluation are synthetic. Results apply only to the simulator, not to real relationship prediction or actual Vouchsafe members.

📊

Monitoring

The policy tracks constraint violations, clarification costs, coverage, and outcome disparities. Failures and uneven outcomes are logged for review.

Assumptions Requiring Validation

Before any real-world use, the following simulator assumptions would need validation:

  • Preference stability over time
  • Accuracy of self-reported constraints
  • Representativeness of synthetic preference distributions
  • Feedback latency patterns in real introduction workflows
  • Acceptability of clarification frequency in practice
